Dates You Can't Move

The gateway is where partner TLS, message signing, and API credentials live. Each of these deadlines lands there first.

Mar 15, 2027

100-day TLS certificates

Public TLS certificates drop from a 200-day maximum to 100 days, then to 47 days in 2029. Certificates renewed by hand twice a year will need renewing about four times a year.

Jan 1, 2027

CNSA 2.0 for new acquisitions

New National Security System purchases are expected to support NSA's quantum-resistant algorithm suite.

Dec 31, 2030

Federal post-quantum migration

Executive Order 14412 moves high-value federal systems to post-quantum key establishment by the end of 2030 and post-quantum signatures by 2031. Their suppliers and partners follow.

Oct 2026

Agents get their own identities

IBM Agent Identity and HashiCorp Vault 2.2 are generally available. Agents moving past pilot now need workload identity, short-lived credentials, and egress control.

Certificate-Lifetime Sprint

We inventory every certificate and key across your DataPower domains and API Connect gateways, flag the public ones, and design automated issuance from Vault PKI or your own CA over ACME, pushed to DataPower through its REST management interface. Then we pilot it on one non-production domain.

Quantum-Safe Gateway Readiness

A cryptographic bill of materials (CBOM) for your gateway tier: DataPower, API Connect, Vault, and partner TLS. We identify which uses are quantum-vulnerable, which long-lived data is exposed to harvest-now-decrypt-later, and where version gaps block the new algorithms.

You get: the CBOM, a crypto-agility design, and a roadmap to the dates your auditors will ask about.

You get: an agent and credential map, a target architecture, and an implementation plan.
