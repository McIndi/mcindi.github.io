      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge">File Operations</span>

      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge">AI Prep</span>

      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge">Development</span>
        <h3>Regex Workbench</h3>
        <p>Live pattern testing with match highlighting and capture group display. Build and validate expressions against real sample data.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge">Documentation</span>
        <h3>Notes Wiki</h3>
        <p>Markdown-based note-taking with CamelCase linking between entries. A personal wiki that lives in your browser with no sync required.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge">Publishing</span>
        <h3>Static Page Generator</h3>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="badge">Security</span>

      <article class="card">
